**Objective:** Generate a high-resolution, photorealistic image by integrating the full model and exact garment from [Model Image] into [Background Image]. The final output must be generated with flawless, pixel-perfect precision across the entire 4K frame, indistinguishable from a raw, unedited photograph taken by a real photographer.

## I. MASTER PIXEL QUALITY & RESOLUTION DIRECTIVES (4K STANDARD)
(Do not change this section. It ensures the professional high-resolution output.)

- **4K Generative Upscaling:** Analyze and render the final composite at a true 4K resolution. Use Sub-Pixel Analysis to sharpen edges and enhance textures based on physics-based logic, not artistic interpolation. Every single pixel must be cohesive and realistic.
- **No-Hallucination Upscaling:** Never create new fabric textures or patterns during the enhancement process. The added high-resolution details must strictly adhere to the original, lower-resolution fabric grain, weave, and colors.
- **Zero Artifacting:** The final image must contain zero compression artifacts, color banding, aliasing, or interpolated "halos" on any edge.

## II. PRODUCT INTEGRITY (MANDATORY LOSSLESS)
(Do not change this section. This is your primary "Immutable Garment" constraint.)

- **Immutable Garment:** Do not change the fabric texture, material type, color HEX codes, or patterns.
- **Detail Preservation:** Maintain 100% of the high-frequency details (e.g., the specific pleats, the knit, the sheer transparency of [Model Image]).
- **Ethnicity & Identity:** The model's ethnicity, hair color/style, and facial features must remain consistent with [Model Image].

## III. DYNAMIC POSE & SURFACE INTERACTION
(Keeps this focused on adaptive posing and surface anchoring.)

- **Adaptive Posing:** Subtly adjust the pose (e.g., weight shift, limb position) to match the perspective and slope of the ground in [Background Image].
- **Surface Physics:** The model must interact with the ground according to its material.
  - If Sand/Soil: The feet must sink slightly; grains gather at the shoe base.
  - If Hard Ground: Apply precise, sharp Ambient Occlusion at the contact points (where the shoe sole makes precise contact).

## IV. MASTER LIGHT & SHADOW CONTROL (v7.0 - CINEMATIC DYNAMICS)
(Overwrites v6.0 directives.)

- **Geometric Scene Analysis:** Identify the established light source AND existing background object shadows. If background chairs cast long shadows to the left, the model's cast shadow must also be long and project to the left.
- **Projected Cast Shadows (Geometric Consistency):** You are prohibited from generating neutral, centered dark patches directly under the feet. You must project a full-form cast shadow across the ground plane. This shadow must match the exact length, angle, and soft-edge blur-radius (diffusion) of other background objects (e.g., lounge chairs), ensuring total geometric harmony across the frame.
- **Micro-Occlusion Grounding:** Use a razor-thin, dense occlusion shadow line only at the precise contact points (where the sole meets the floor).
- **Directional Form Lighting:** Discard studio lighting. Re-light the model from the directional background source. Apply realistic "bounce light" (e.g., golden hour bounce from sand) to the shadowed side.
